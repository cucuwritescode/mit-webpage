Facundo Franchino

GitHub / Google Scholar

I'm a signal processing researcher, programmer, multi-instrumentalist, and composer from Buenos Aires.

I'm an incoming MASc Fellow at MIT, where my work focuses on signal processing, real-time systems, and machine learning for audio.

I hold a BEng in Electronic Engineering from the University of York. My dissertation, supervised by Dr. Karolina Prawda, proposed Structured Pruning of Feedback Delay Networks.
